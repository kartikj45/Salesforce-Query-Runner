Privacy Policy for SF Query Runner
Last Updated: November 2026
SF Query Runner (“the Extension”) is designed to help users import and export data within Salesforce. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

Data We AccessTo perform import and export operations, the Extension accesses:• Authentication Information: Your active Salesforce session cookie (sid) to authenticate API requests directly with your logged-in Salesforce instance.• Salesforce Data: Object metadata and records you explicitly choose to query, export, or import.• Local Preferences: Extension settings and saved field mappings stored locally in your browser using Chrome Storage.
How Your Data Is Used and Stored• All data processing happens locally within your browser and directly between your browser and official Salesforce servers (*.salesforce.com, *.force.com).• No External Servers: We do not collect, transmit, store, or log your Salesforce credentials, session tokens, or record data on any external developer or third-party servers.• No Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.
Permissions Used• cookies & tabs: Used strictly to detect your active Salesforce org domain and authenticate API requests on your behalf.• storage: Used to save your local extension settings and mappings in your browser.
